🎮 How to Play

Objective

Survive as long as possible by defeating waves of zombies. Defeat all zombies to progress!

🕹️ Controls

  • W/A/S/D - Move around
  • Arrow Keys - Alternative movement
  • Mouse - Aim your weapon
  • Left Click - Shoot
  • R Key - Reload (auto-reload at 0 ammo)

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Keep moving! Don't stay in one spot
  • Use corners to your advantage
  • Group zombies together for easy kills
  • Monitor your health carefully
  • Ammo regenerates when depleted
  • Aim for the head (center of zombie)

🧟 Enemy Info

  • Zombies spawn from map edges
  • Each zombie takes 2-3 headshots
  • Avoid direct contact - it damages you
  • More zombies appear as you get kills

⚠️ Health System

  • You start with 100 HP
  • Each zombie hit deals 10-15 damage
  • No healing items available
  • Game ends when health reaches 0

🎯 Scoring

  • +1 Kill per zombie defeated
  • Survival time increases over time
  • Difficulty scales with kills
  • Try to beat your high score!
